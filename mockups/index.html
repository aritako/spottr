Quiet Ledger

A cool graphite take on the same calm idea, closer to an architect’s notebook than a wellness app. Schibsted Grotesk keeps the voice human but unsentimental; Spline Sans Mono takes every weight, rep and estimate. Structure comes from hairlines and a restrained 10–14px radius, not from cards stacked on cards. The orange is burnt and quiet, reserved for the live set, the PR and the one primary button.

Warm Nocturne

The premium-feeling version that isn’t afraid of pleasure: espresso-charcoal layers, 20–28px corners, and air you can feel between elements. Bricolage Grotesque gives headlines a little swagger without shouting; Martian Mono makes the numbers feel stamped into metal. Persimmon orange appears as a candle rather than a flare—mostly around the active set, the rest ring, and the PR. This is the closest to a members-club or boutique-hotel mood.

Editorial Measure

A high-contrast didone used as punctuation, not decoration: one large number or line per view, then quiet Archivo for the working interface. This direction borrows from fashion and financial publishing—double rules, margins, footnote citations in the coach—rather than from wellness. The orange is a hotter flare, used like a printer’s mark: PR diamonds, active rules, a single action. It is the most opinionated of the three, and the least like the original C.
